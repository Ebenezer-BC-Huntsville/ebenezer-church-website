The Gospel

Colors of the Gospel

[REPLACE: one or two sentences introducing why the church tells the gospel story this way.]

  • Black — Sin, which separates us from God.
    "For all have sinned and fall short of the glory of God." Romans 3:23
  • Red — The blood of Jesus, shed for our sins.
    "For God so loved the world, that he gave his only Son, that whoever believes in him should not perish but have eternal life." John 3:16
  • White — We are clean and forgiven through Christ.
    "Wash me, and I shall be whiter than snow." Psalms 51:7
  • Green — Growing in God's Word.
    "But grow in the grace and knowledge of our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ." 2 Peter 3:18
  • Gold — Heaven, where God lives.
    "The city was pure gold, like unto clear glass." Revelation 21:18
